Located in West Hongqiao, the ANTA Shanghai Operations Center is set to become the ANTA Group's new office landmark. Mlesun Group provided the office and dining furniture solutions, injecting the brand's athletic DNA into spatial details — furniture that carries efficient collaboration and energetic co-creation, keeping the space in tune with the brand spirit of "Keep Moving."

With craftsmanship and smart solutions — from individual workstations to public amenities, from meeting rooms to the staff canteen — Mlesun delivered furniture for every scenario as one integrated package, creating a modern office space that blends function, design, and culture for one of China's leading sportswear brands.
