Taikang Home · Bei Qing Fu is the newest "Fu Series" care-focused senior living community launched by Taikang Group in Changping District, Beijing — a medically integrated nursing home centered on care for residents with disabilities and cognitive (memory) disorders.

Mlesun provided a complete, full-scenario furniture solution for Bei Qing Fu, customizing furniture to suit seniors' behavioral habits and care needs. Balancing senior-safe design, everyday functionality, and spatial aesthetics, every space is crafted to carry a free, dignified, and joyful later life.

01

Reception Hall

Elegant seating that conveys the warmth of care

A stately yet welcoming lounge sofa and waiting furniture set — an open space with a warm, refined feel that accommodates visitor reception, family waiting, and first consultations, projecting Taikang Home's professional and caring brand character from the very first glance.

02

Café

A relaxed corner for unhurried chats with old friends

Casual, easy-going furniture in a scattered layout, with small tables and soft chairs creating a light atmosphere — a dedicated social corner where residents can chat with old friends, read, take a quiet rest, or enjoy afternoon tea, enriching daily social life.

03

Multi-Function Hall

A venue for diverse activities

A flexible furniture scheme: tables and chairs can be quickly rearranged for lectures, holiday events, choir rehearsals, group activities and more. One room, many uses — hosting a rich variety of cultural and recreational time for residents.

04

Pet Therapy Zone

Gentle companions that soothe the heart

Soft, home-like furniture fits the therapy scene: low, rounded-corner upholstered pieces with comfortable seating let residents interact up close with therapy pets in a relaxed setting — easing emotions and gaining gentle, healing comfort.

05

Game Therapy Zone

Seated interaction — joy through play

Enclosed casual tables and chairs, height-matched to seniors' seated posture, make it easy to run cognitive board games and group craft activities — exercising cognitive ability through relaxed, happy play that calms the mind and lifts the spirit.

06

Five-Senses Therapy Zone

Soft furniture, sensory soothing

Low-stimulation, highly inclusive therapeutic furniture — simple forms, gentle colors, no sharp edges — combined with multi-sensory interventions (sound, light, touch) to gently settle emotions and support professional care for residents with cognitive impairment.

07

Rehabilitation & Clinic Area

Training and rest combined, safe recovery

Supportive rehab furniture and rest seating match the rhythm of therapy sessions, allowing residents to rest safely at any moment between exercises. Clean, simple clinical furniture with a soft, non-oppressive feel reduces medical anxiety and creates a warmer care space.

08

Memory Town

Recreating the old days, awakening warm memories

Bespoke furniture for nostalgic-themed scenes recreates period life vignettes, building a familiar and reassuring atmosphere that helps residents with dementia awaken warm memories — a healing space full of belonging.

09

Dining Area

Ergonomic tables and chairs for safe, comfortable meals

Mlesun's ergonomic dining tables and chairs, optimized specifically for seniors: a stable center of gravity, anti-slip and anti-tip design, and armrests that provide support — making it easier to sit down and stand up, so every meal is comfortable and safe.

10

Nursing Rooms

Senior-friendly details that guard dignified daily care

Entryway seating and storage furniture designed for seniors: seat height matched for easy rising, with armrests for support — making shoe changing stable and effortless, and creating the first safe buffer of "coming home." A full suite of custom senior-care nursing furniture: fully rounded anti-collision design and stable supporting structures, convenient for caregivers to operate while supporting residents' needs to lean on and rise with help — safeguarding residents' living dignity on top of professional care.
